# Delivery Friction & Savings Calculator

A simple calculator that helps B2B SaaS and Fintech leaders put a number on the friction in their client onboarding and implementation delivery, and estimate what fixing it could save.

Built by [Enwise Insights & Advisory](https://enwiseinsight.com), a London-based practice helping B2B Fintech and SaaS companies scale professional services without scaling costs at the same rate.

## Why this exists

Deals are closing, but onboarding is slow, implementation teams are stretched, and costs are rising. Delivery friction is usually treated as a capacity problem, when it is often a symptom of deeper causes: bespoke implementations, inconsistent processes, knowledge silos and unclear client expectations.

Those costs are rarely measured. This calculator makes them visible, so the conversation about fixing them starts from numbers instead of gut feel.

## What it does

- Captures where delivery friction shows up in your implementations
- Estimates the cost of that friction
- Estimates the potential savings from reducing it

## Who it is for

- CEOs, COOs and CCOs at growing B2B SaaS and Fintech companies
- Heads of Professional Services, Delivery, Implementation and Customer Success
- Advisors and fractional leaders who need a quick way to frame the business case for change
